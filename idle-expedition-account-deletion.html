Idle Expedition - Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how players can request deletion of their Idle Expedition account data.

Developer Information

Developer: Ringo Studio

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your Idle Expedition account data, please contact us at:

bmringo01@gmail.com

Please use the subject:

Idle Expedition - Data Deletion Request

To help us identify your account, please include:

  • Your Unity Authentication email address (if applicable)
  • Your Player ID (if available)

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of the request, Ringo Studio will delete data associated with your use of Idle Expedition, including:

  • Game progression and account progress
  • Characters and inventory data
  • Cloud Save data
  • Player profile information stored by Idle Expedition
  • Other gameplay-related data associated with your account

Important Note

Idle Expedition uses Unity Authentication for player sign-in. Deletion of your Idle Expedition data does not delete your Unity account itself. If you wish to manage or delete your Unity account, please refer to Unity's account management services.
