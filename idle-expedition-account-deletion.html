Idle Expedition Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Idle Expedition account and associated data.

Developer Information

Developer: Ringo Studio

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please send an email to:

bmringo01@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your Player ID (if available)
  • Your Unity Account email address
  • The subject line "Account Deletion Request"

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Game progress
  • Character data
  • Inventory data
  • Cloud save data
  • Associated account information used by Idle Expedition

Data Retention

Backup data may be retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.
